## 2. Key case: SKU001 demand jump

Weekly sales W35-W38: 185, 197, 186, 186. W39-W42: 261, 267, 257, 259. Sales stepped up about 40% at W39 and stayed there.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-W39 | 207.5 | 197.83 | 1.049 | no | 3.64 | STOCKOUT_RISK | 500 |
| 2026-W40 | 225.0 | 204.42 | 1.101 | no | 3.52 | STOCKOUT_RISK | 500 |
| 2026-W41 | 242.75 | 208.83 | 1.162 | no | 3.45 | STOCKOUT_RISK | 500 |
| 2026-W42 | 261.0 | 214.92 | 1.214 | yes | 3.35 | STOCKOUT_RISK | 750 |

**Finding:** the high-demand rule compares a 4-week average with a 12-week average that already contains those 4 weeks, so a sustained step change only crosses the 1.20 threshold in the fourth week after it starts (W42). The stockout rule caught SKU001 from the first replayed run, so the reorder was never missed, but for three runs it recommended the standard 500 units instead of 750. Raghav may want to review the 1.20 threshold or compare avg4 with the 12 weeks *before* them.

## 4. What this backtest cannot validate

- **Stock history.** There is one inventory snapshot (as of 2026-10-19). Every replayed run uses that same stock figure, so the cover numbers for W39-W41 are not what the business actually had then. The flags show how the *sales signal* evolved, not real past decisions.
- **Festival uplift accuracy.** The sample has 16 weeks (W27-W42, July to mid-October 2026). The only festival month it touches is October 2026, which is only partly observed, and there is no earlier festival period to compare against. Whether +25% is the right uplift cannot be tested with this data.
- **Outcomes.** There is no record of what was actually ordered, delivered or stocked out, so the backtest cannot score the recommendations as right or wrong.
- **Open purchase orders.** Zoho is not connected yet, so stock already on order is not counted in cover. A SKU with a large PO arriving next week may be flagged as a stockout risk.
- **Scale.** The sample has 15 SKUs and 4 distributors. Performance at real scale is covered separately by a synthetic 400 SKU x 156 week test, not by real data.
- **Forecasting.** No forecasting model was built or tested. Sixteen weeks of history cannot support one; it remains a future option once more history exists.
